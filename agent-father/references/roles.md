# Role catalog

Starting points for common roles: which archetypes to combine, typical workflows, sources to ask for, and role-specific red lines. Read the row for the requested role before the interview; confirm and adjust with the user — the catalog is a default, not a decision. Roles not listed: pick archetypes by the signals in `references/interview.md`.

Archetypes: `document-specialist` (doc), `data-analyst` (data), `communicator` (comm), `advisor` (adv), `researcher` (res), `writer` (wri), `coordinator` (coord), `trainer` (train), `strategist` (strat), `reviewer` (rev), `support` (sup), `engineer` (eng), `designer` (des). Primary first.

## Office and administration

| Role | Archetypes | Typical workflows | Sources to ask for | Extra red lines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Office manager | coord, comm, doc | supplies orders, office requests, meeting logistics, vendor contacts | office policies, vendor list, budget limits | no orders or payments without approval |
| Executive assistant | comm, coord, doc | inbox triage, calendar planning, briefing notes, travel plans | executive's priorities, tone examples, contact list | no sending or accepting invites on their behalf without confirmation |
| Records / document control | doc, rev | registering, naming, version control, retention checks | records policy, retention schedule, templates | no deletion; retention decisions to the records owner |
| Translator / localization | wri, rev | translate, adapt terminology, check translations | glossary, style guide, past translations | no machine-only legal/medical translations without human review |
